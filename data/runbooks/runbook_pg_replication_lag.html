RB-206: PostgreSQL Replica Lag

Serviceorders-db replica db-replica-02 (primary db-primary-01)
SeveritySEV-3 at warning, SEV-2 at critical
OwnerData Platform team
Last reviewed2026-10-08

1. Symptoms

  • Alert PostgresReplicaLagHigh fires at warning when replay lag on db-replica-02 exceeds 64 MB for 5 minutes, and at critical above 256 MB or a replay_lag above 120 seconds.
  • Customers do not see an order they just placed: reads through the orders-db-ro endpoint are stale.
  • The RB-204 failover pre-check (lag under 16 MB) fails.

2. Impact and automatic protection

At critical, the pgbouncer health check removes db-replica-02 from the orders-db-ro read pool and read traffic falls back to db-primary-01. That keeps reads correct but adds about 30% CPU on the primary, so a critical lag must be fixed within 1 hour, before the nightly batch at 01:00 UTC.

3. Diagnosis

3.1 Measure the lag from the primary

On db-primary-01:

sudo -u postgres psql -xc "SELECT application_name, state, sent_lsn, replay_lsn,
  pg_size_pretty(pg_wal_lsn_diff(pg_current_wal_lsn(), replay_lsn)) AS replay_bytes,
  replay_lag FROM pg_stat_replication;"

If sent_lsn is close to the current LSN but replay_lsn is far behind, the replica is slow to apply. If sent_lsn itself is behind, the network is the bottleneck.

3.2 Check the WAL receiver on the replica

On db-replica-02:

sudo -u postgres psql -xc "SELECT status, sender_host, last_msg_receipt_time,
  latest_end_lsn FROM pg_stat_wal_receiver;"

status must be streaming. A last_msg_receipt_time older than 30 seconds means the replication connection is stuck; no row at all means the receiver is down.

4. Remediation

4.1 Cancel long-running queries on the replica

sudo -u postgres psql -c "SELECT pid, now() - query_start AS age, left(query, 60)
  FROM pg_stat_activity WHERE state = 'active' AND now() - query_start > interval '5 minutes';"
sudo -u postgres psql -c "SELECT pg_cancel_backend(<PID>);"

The usual offender is the nightly_revenue_export report; ask Data Platform to move it to the analytics warehouse.

4.2 Relieve network saturation

Confirm with sar -n DEV 1 10 on db-replica-02. The common cause is the weekly base backup copying over the same link: pause it with sudo systemctl stop walg-backup.service and restart it after the lag is under 16 MB.

4.3 Relieve disk I/O pressure

Check for a running VACUUM or pg_repack on the replica host and stop it. If await stays above 20 ms with nothing else running, the NVMe drive may be failing: page the Storage team.

4.4 Rebuild a replica that fell too far behind

If the replica logs requested WAL segment has already been removed, or lag is above 50 GB, rebuild it from the primary:

sudo -u postgres pg_basebackup -h db-primary-01 -D /var/lib/postgresql/16/main \
  -X stream -P -R -c fast 2>&1 | tee /tmp/basebackup.log

A rebuild of the 1.2 TB cluster takes about 2 hours.

Warning: do not promote a lagging replica, even if the primary looks unhealthy. Every transaction not yet replayed on db-replica-02 is lost permanently. Promote only when lag is under 16 MB, as RB-204 requires.

5. Verification

  • replay_bytes in pg_stat_replication stays under 16 MB for 15 minutes.
  • PostgresReplicaLagHigh resolves, and pgbouncer shows db-replica-02 back in the orders-db-ro pool (SHOW SERVERS; on the pgbouncer admin console).
