RB-603: STUCK DEPLOYMENT ROLLOUT
================================

Service:        payments-api (k8s namespace: payments)
Severity:       SEV-2
Owner:          Kubernetes Platform team
Last reviewed:  2026-10-20


1. SYMPTOMS
-----------

  * Alert KubeDeploymentRolloutStuck fires when a deployment reports
    Progressing=False with reason ProgressDeadlineExceeded.
  * Alert KubeDeploymentReplicasMismatch fires when updated replicas differ
    from desired replicas for 15 minutes.
  * Argo CD shows the application payments-api as "Progressing" or
    "Degraded" instead of "Healthy".


2. HOW PAYMENTS-API IS DEPLOYED
-------------------------------

  Argo CD syncs payments-api from the payments-deploy git repo with
  auto-sync and selfHeal enabled; it checks git every 3 minutes. The
  deployment runs 6 replicas with maxSurge 25% and maxUnavailable 0, so old
  pods keep serving traffic while new ones fail. progressDeadlineSeconds is
  600: after 10 minutes without progress the rollout is marked as failed,
  but Kubernetes does not roll it back by itself.


3. DIAGNOSIS
------------

  Find out why the new ReplicaSet cannot become ready. The two usual causes
  are an image that cannot be pulled and a readiness probe that never
  passes. Both leave the old ReplicaSet running at full size.

3.1 Check the rollout state

    kubectl -n payments rollout status deployment payments-api --timeout=30s
    kubectl -n payments get rs -l app=payments-api
    kubectl -n payments get pods -l app=payments-api

  The newest ReplicaSet has pods that are not READY; the STATUS column
  tells you which of the next two subsections applies.

3.2 Pods in ImagePullBackOff

    kubectl -n payments describe pod <POD_NAME> | grep -A3 Failed

  "manifest unknown" means the tag was never pushed to
  registry.internal.example.com; "401 Unauthorized" means the pull secret
  regcred in namespace payments has expired. The kubelet retries the pull
  with a back-off of up to 5 minutes.

3.3 Readiness probe failing

  Pods are Running with 0/1 READY. The probe calls GET /healthz/ready on
  port 8080 every 10 seconds and marks the pod unready after 3 failures.
  Check the events and the application log:

    kubectl -n payments describe pod <POD_NAME> | grep "Readiness probe"
    kubectl -n payments logs <POD_NAME> --tail=50


4. REMEDIATION
--------------

  The fastest fix is to roll back to the last good revision, and then fix
  the change in git. Follow the three steps below in order.

4.1 Pause Argo CD auto-sync

    argocd app set payments-api --sync-policy none

  WARNING: do not roll back while auto-sync is still on. Argo CD sees the
  live state drift from git and re-applies the broken revision within 3
  minutes, undoing your rollback.

4.2 Roll back to a known good revision

    kubectl -n payments rollout history deployment payments-api
    kubectl -n payments rollout undo deployment payments-api --to-revision=<N>

  Pick the revision explicitly with --to-revision. Without it, undo only
  goes back one revision, which may be another bad one.

4.3 Revert in git and re-enable auto-sync

  Revert the bad commit in payments-deploy, wait until the Argo CD diff for
  payments-api is empty, then turn auto-sync back on:

    argocd app set payments-api --sync-policy automated --self-heal


5. VERIFICATION
---------------

  * kubectl -n payments rollout status deployment payments-api reports
    "successfully rolled out".
  * All 6 pods are READY 1/1 and the old ReplicaSet is scaled to 0.
  * Argo CD shows payments-api as "Synced" and "Healthy".
